About

I study the intricate neural circuits underlying innate behavior, seeking to understand how the brain's architecture gives rise to complex patterns of action.

My work spans electrophysiological mechanisms for neural plasticity and the development of novel tools for systems neuroscience research. I believe that understanding the brain requires both precise measurement and creative thinking.

Previously at NYU's Neuroscience Institute, I developed approaches bridging computational and experimental neuroscience. Now at MIT, I continue pushing the boundaries of what we can observe and understand about neural computation.

Research

Neural Circuits & Innate Behavior

Investigating the hardwired neural pathways that produce complex innate behaviors, from sensory integration to motor output.

Electrophysiological Mechanisms

Studying how synaptic plasticity and neural dynamics give rise to learning and memory at the circuit level.

Novel Tools for Neuroscience

Developing new experimental and computational tools to observe, perturb, and understand neural systems at unprecedented resolution.
